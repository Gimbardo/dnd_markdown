## Actions

- **Battleaxe**: Melee Weapon Attack: +8 to hit, 2d8+5 danni
- **Demonic Hand**: Ranged: +7 to hit, 4d6 fire damage

## Legendary Actions (1/fight)

- **Misty Step** 20ft range (darkness flavored)

## Lair Actions

Initiative 20 start effect, next Initiative 20 triggers

- **Fire Columns**: 6 square 10x10 start boiling fire from below, trigger: a column of lava erupts, dealing 3d10 fire damage
- **Winged Demon**: Izek Reveals his wings, creating an area around himself of 15 feet of necrotic energy, trigger: deals 2d10 necrotic daamage to those in the circle
- **Imp Eggs**: Izec Spawns 4 eggs around the arena (CA10, 1HP), trigger: every egg alive spawns an [Imp](https://5e.tools/bestiary.html#imp_mm) that takes its turn after Izec
